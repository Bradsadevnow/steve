# Credits

## Music engine: The Whimsy Machine, by Andrew Edmark

Steve's music is powered by **The Whimsy Machine**, a deterministic phrase-to-music engine built entirely by
**Andrew Edmark** (https://oopismcgoopis.com/whimsy/). A typed phrase becomes a creature and a six-voice chamber ensemble
through programs written in LOLCODE, Malbolge, Brainfuck, Befunge, Piet, FRACTRAN, the Shakespeare Programming Language,
INTERCAL and Inform 7. All of that design and code is his work, used here with his permission. Source:
`third_party/whimsy-machine/` (see `NOTICE.md`).
